Welcome to GenZ Loan. By using our application, you agree to comply with and be bound by the following terms and conditions of use.

We respect your privacy. We collect biometric data and personal information solely for identity verification and loan qualification purposes. Your data is encrypted with 256-bit security.

Interest rates are dynamically calculated between 8% and 15%. Repayment periods range from 21 to 90 days depending on the loan size.

A non-refundable processing fee (equivalent to 500 KES) is required for each loan disbursement to cover administrative costs.
